Data flow: how 1391 cues move through seven steps

Counts from dog-man-2025 (1391 cues, run with --audio). Each column is one status: a step processes the statuses its box spans and the others pass straight by, so every step keeps 1391 cues in total. The 108 outliers demoted by 02: 05 rescues 16, 04's audio check keeps 6, and the other 86 end as outlier (including the 3 verified cues 06 sends back). PLACED = anchored, verified, rescued. 04 runs only with --audio and only checks outliers of at least 3 words; 06 turns out-of-order PLACED cues back into outlier and re-interpolates them at once. 07 changes timing only, never status.
